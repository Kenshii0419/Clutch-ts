Seller Guidelines

These guidelines keep YouMadeIt warm, authentic, and reliable for every Iloilo maker and shopper.

1. Handmade & original creations

  • List items crafted, illustrated, designed, or assembled by you or your small local studio—no mass-produced reselling or dropshipping.
  • Describe materials, dimensions, processing times, and personalization options clearly.
  • Price listings accurately in Philippine pesos (₱) and upload clear, true-to-life photos (JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 4 MB).

2. Moderation & review

  • New listings and major listing edits go through administrator review before appearing publicly in search and storefront grids.

3. Fulfilment & communication

  • Keep stock counts accurate to avoid overselling.
  • Mark orders as processing or shipped promptly and include tracking numbers when dispatched.
  • Respond kindly to buyer inquiries sent through your Seller Studio.
