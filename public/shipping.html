Shipping & Local Delivery

How makers fulfil orders

Each artist packs and dispatches orders from their own studio. Processing times and delivery notes appear on each product page and shop storefront.

Iloilo & Philippine deliveries

  • Local Iloilo City & nearby municipality orders can be sent via local courier or coordinated for meetup when supported by the maker.
  • Nationwide orders across the Philippines ship via standard local couriers with tracking numbers recorded in My Orders.
