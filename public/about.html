Our Story

Made by local hands. Shared with the community.

YouMadeIt is a cozy online marketplace celebrating independent artists, crafters, illustrators, weavers, potters, and small handmade businesses across Iloilo, Philippines.

Inspired by community art fairs and neighborhood craft markets, we built YouMadeIt so local makers have an inviting place to showcase their stories, connect directly with thoughtful shoppers, and grow creative livelihoods without feeling lost inside massive corporate catalogs.

Community Trust

New accounts and listings go through marketplace review so buyers and makers alike enjoy a welcoming, honest space.

Rooted in Iloilo

Every purchase supports local creativity—from ceramic mugs thrown in small studios to handwoven goods and pressed floral stationery.
