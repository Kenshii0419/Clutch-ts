Returns & Refunds

Maker shop policies

Each independent maker publishes their own return policy directly on their shop storefront. Always check custom/personalized item rules before ordering.

When orders can be cancelled

  • Buyers can cancel an order from My Orders while its status is pending payment or paid (before shipping).
  • Cancelling restores item inventory back to the maker’s listing automatically.

Damaged or incorrect items

If a handmade piece arrives damaged in transit, message the maker directly or reach out through Contact Support with your order number.
