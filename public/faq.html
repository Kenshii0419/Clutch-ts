Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for shoppers and local Iloilo creators.

How do I purchase handmade items?

Create an account, confirm your email, and once approved you can add creations to your cart or use Buy Now. Prices are shown in Philippine pesos (₱).

How do payments currently work?

Checkout currently uses a sandbox test flow for demonstration and verification. No live payment gateway or real charge occurs.

How long does order preparation take?

Each maker sets their own processing time on their storefront and product details—typically 1 to 3 business days, or longer for made-to-order pieces.

How do I leave a product review?

After the maker marks your order as shipped and you click "Confirm Order Received" on My Orders, the verified review form unlocks on that product’s page.

How do I open a seller shop?

Sign in with an approved account and visit Seller Studio to choose your shop name, URL slug, and studio location.

How do I message an artist?

Use the "Message Maker" button on any product detail page or shop storefront.
