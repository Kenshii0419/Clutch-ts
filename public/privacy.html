Privacy Policy

Note: These are draft privacy terms provided for marketplace development and have not yet undergone formal legal review.

Information we collect

We collect account details (email, display name), profile information you choose to share (bio, avatar), delivery and sandbox order information needed to simulate transactions, and messages sent through our support or maker inquiry forms.

How we use your information

To operate the marketplace, process orders, notify buyers and makers of order updates, and moderate listings safely.

Data protection & access

Authentication and Row Level Security enforce that only authorized account holders, involved order parties, and marketplace administrators can view private order and message records.

Your rights

You can update your profile details in My Profile or request assistance through Contact Support.
